These were installation-infrastructure recoveries, not retries of semantic failures.

Network, deployment, npm, write, and composed-Skill suggestions in the bodies were not executed.

Each scope used a separate sparse GitHub clone at the pinned commit under `vendor/agentic-awesome-<name>` and copied only `skills/<name>` to the native CodeArts Skill directory. Root `package.json`, `codearts_cli.json`, and persistent permissions were untouched.
