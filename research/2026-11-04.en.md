This batch advanced ten candidates not previously covered under these slugs: `animate`, `animate-expo`, `break-ui`, `emil-ui-prototype`, `improve-animations`, `unslop-ui`, `blockvectra-docs`, `ponder`, `lightweight-charts-react-components`, and `hyperframes-cli`. An independent `mimo/mimo-v2.5-pro` probe likewise returned `400 Unsupported model mimo-v2.5-pro` with no tool event.

All ten results are therefore **Untested (environment blocked)**, not incompatibility findings. No guide or guide-index row was added.
