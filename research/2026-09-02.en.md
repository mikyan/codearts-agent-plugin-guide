## Candidate results

1. **`brainstorm-ideas-new` — Partial:** projects A/B each called only the target skill and produced five ideas from each PM, Designer, and Engineer perspective plus a Top 5. User scope completed discovery and loading but produced no final text before the 90-second limit; exit was recorded as 124. User-scope core behavior did not reproduce, so there is no guide.
2. **`prioritize-assumptions` — Works:** all scopes mapped A/B/C/D to test/proceed/reject/defer and gave A a calendar-authorization behavior experiment with a 25% threshold. User scope initially failed the literal assertion because it omitted suffixes such as `TEST-FIRST=A`; the raw result table unambiguously showed A → TEST-FIRST and every other mapping. This was an auditable false negative, not a retry.
3. **`opportunity-solution-tree` — Works:** all scopes produced one 7-day activation metric, at least three opportunities, at least three solutions for each prioritized opportunity, and an experiment with metric and threshold.
4. **`product-vision` — Works:** all scopes produced three one-sentence visions, a selection, and value alignment. Project A emitted three internal CodeArts `todowrite` completions in addition to the target skill; they only tracked task state and had no network or file side effect.
5. **`value-proposition` — Works:** all scopes covered Who, Why, What Before, How, What After, and Alternatives, followed by concise value-proposition and positioning statements.
6. **`lean-canvas` — Works:** all scopes covered the nine Lean Canvas areas and added key assumptions and validation experiments.
7. **`swot-analysis` — Works:** all scopes produced at least three items in each quadrant and at least three prioritized actions, using only prompt-supplied facts.
8. **`porters-five-forces` — Works:** all scopes rated all five forces, identified the top two, and assessed attractiveness without live market research.
9. **`stakeholder-map` — Works:** all scopes placed the four supplied roles in the correct quadrants and gave frequency, channel, message, neglect risk, and a conflict-alignment action.
10. **`user-stories` — Works:** all scopes produced exactly three stories, each with title, As a/I want/so that, Design N/A, four acceptance criteria, and an INVEST check. The literal assertion required `DESIGN=N/A`, while outputs used `Design: N/A` or localized labels. Raw text proved the full structure, so manual audit corrected the false negative.
