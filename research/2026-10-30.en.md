# 2026-10-30: Ten CodeArts validations on Agentic Awesome v20.2.0

[简体中文](2026-10-30.md)

## Summary

This batch advanced ten content-only skills not previously covered under these slugs: `bifrost-gateway`, `comment-to-dm-cta`, `web-design-system`, `web-review`, `ai-content-improver`, `blog-title-generator`, `headline-analyzer`, `keyword-clustering-tool`, `readability-enhancer`, and `faq-generator`. The source was pinned to Agentic Awesome `v20.2.0` at `5cd587241fdaf6d111e4ea00be899b55cc77ff08`.

Each candidate independently went through source-hash verification, unique CodeArts discovery, a real `codearts run`, exact removal, and post-removal discovery in project A, a fresh project B, and user scope. Installation passed 30/30, discovery passed 30/30, and rollback passed 30/30. However, all 30 `mimo/mimo-v2.5` calls returned `400 Unsupported model mimo-v2.5` before any `skill` or other tool event. An independent probe of the only configured alternative, `mimo/mimo-v2.5-pro`, was also blocked by network policy before any tool event.

All ten results are therefore **Untested (environment blocked)**. This is not evidence that the candidates are incompatible. No guide or guide-index row was added.

## Rollback and publication decision

All 30 post-removal discovery checks excluded the former absolute path, and all ten user skill directories were absent afterward. These non-secret hashes were identical before and after the batch:

- `package.json`: `589A2F93D6B401FCCE4EDED58C4C477C3891C3B580796862C58ACB4E5D73A4E7`
- `codearts_cli.json`: `6F08A23430F657E3CBF75ABDCF1CB7A08FE88504F6FDC51BA31B6530386E2E23`
- persistent permission file: `AC7831CD9D9FB18023BFD6C7778E71FF4A0DBCADAE60948189FBB0ECFDE2F447`

None of the candidates passed runtime loading, representative invocation, or clean-project end-to-end reproduction, so no formal guide was created. When a configured model becomes usable, all 30 candidate/scope calls must be rerun; discovery evidence from this batch cannot be promoted into a compatibility claim.
